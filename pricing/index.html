Sketch CFD pricing

Sketch CFD is free to download and to use with its free features. Sketch CFD Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, with a free trial for new subscribers.

Free and Sketch CFD Pro

Free

  • Run and explore all 14 examples.
  • Draw walls of any shape in a wind tunnel (200 mm × 100 mm of air).
  • Coarse and Medium resolution.
  • Projects, files, and the help.

Sketch CFD Pro adds

  • Design anything: inlets, outlets, fans, heaters, gas sources, and probes, in any size.
  • Gases and liquids, heat and buoyancy, and particles.
  • Fine meshes for more detail.
  • Edit the examples.
  • Import your own paper drawings from a photo.
  • Reports with the run settings and probe readings.

In the iPhone and iPad app

  • Yearly: US$49.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial.
  • Monthly: US$4.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

These are the prices in the United States. In other countries the App Store shows the price in your currency, and it may include taxes. Apple bills the subscription to your Apple Account, and free trials are for eligible new subscribers. Refunds for purchases in the app are handled by Apple.

On the web

The Sketch CFD web app runs in a web browser on a computer. It is not open to the public yet. When it opens, Sketch CFD Pro on the web will cost:

  • Yearly: US$49.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial.
  • Monthly: US$4.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

On the web, Sketch CFD Pro also includes parameter sweeps, optimization studies and designs of experiments, and scorecards and comparisons of sketches.

Orders on the web are processed by Paddle.com, our online reseller and the Merchant of Record for those orders. Sales tax or VAT is added at checkout where it applies. You can get a full refund within 14 days of your first payment and of each yearly renewal, as the Refund Policy says.

Canceling

You can cancel at any time. Sketch CFD Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and your sketches still open and run after it ends. In the app, cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On the web, cancel with the link in your order email, or email support@journeyon.co.
